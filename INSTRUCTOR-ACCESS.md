# Instructor Answer Key — access

An **Instructor Answer Key & Grading Guide** exists for all 30 labs. It contains
the Capture-the-Flag flag answers, guided-track checkpoints, and grading notes.

To protect the integrity of the exercises, the answer key is **not published in
this repository** and is shared only with verified instructors.

## How to request it
Request access through the repository (open an issue, or use the maintainer's
GitHub profile), identifying yourself with your **institutional email address**,
and include:

1. Your name and the institution you teach at.
2. A link to your faculty/staff directory page, or your department/course page.
3. The course or program where you intend to use TCL.

Once your faculty status is confirmed, you'll receive the **password-protected
answer key (PDF)** and its password.

## Please
- Keep the key and password confidential; do not post either publicly or share
  with students.
- Some answers depend on your specific deployment (image versions, scan output) —
  the key marks those `[verify]`.

> Students: the labs are designed to be solved without the key. Use the Guided
> track (6A) and the Hints box (6B) first.
