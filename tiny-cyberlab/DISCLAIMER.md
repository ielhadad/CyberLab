Tiny CyberLab (TCL) is an **educational cyber range**. It intentionally installs
and uses real offensive security tools (Nmap, Metasploit, sqlmap, Ettercap,
BeEF, the Social-Engineer Toolkit, and others) and deliberately vulnerable
targets.

**Use TCL only in an environment and manner you are authorized to use.**

- TCL is designed to run as a **self-contained lab** on an isolated Docker
  bridge network inside a single VM. Keep it that way. Do not bridge it to a
  production, campus, or public network.
- Every technique in these labs must be practiced **only** against the targets
  provided inside the range, or against systems you own or have **explicit,
  written permission** to test.
