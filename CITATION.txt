abstract: >-
  An open-source cyber range for hands-on cybersecurity courses. One script
  builds an isolated network on VirtualBox or AWS EC2: a Kali attacker, a
  firewalled DMZ (OWASP Juice Shop, DVWA), a pivot-only LAN (Metasploitable 2)
  and a Suricata IDS, with a built-in self-test and one-step reset to support
  project-based learning mapped to NICE Framework work roles.
